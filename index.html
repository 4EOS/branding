Logo

Minimum display height is 38 px in UI and 72 px in document headers and print. Only navy and white backgrounds are approved. Never stretch, crop, or recolor the logo, and never place it on yellow or mid gray.

Approved placements: navy (#003D6C) and white (#FFFFFF).

Rules

The short version. When two rules conflict, contrast wins.

Do

  • Build from the core four: navy, blue, yellow, dark base.
  • Set headings in Bahnschrift and body text in Calibri.
  • Reserve yellow for emphasis, CTAs, highlights, and section labels.
  • Fall back to Segoe UI where Bahnschrift is unavailable.

Don't

  • Introduce accent colors outside this palette.
  • Set yellow text on white; it fails contrast.
  • Use serif fonts. The 4EOS identity is sans-serif throughout.
  • Stretch, crop, or recolor the logo.
